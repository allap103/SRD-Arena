# Rules Interpretations

## Creature Space in Three Dimensions

The rules define only the width of a creature's space. SRD Arena uses the
following cubic volumes as its three-dimensional abstraction:

| Size | Defined ground space | SRD Arena 3D abstraction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tiny | 2½ × 2½ ft.; four per square | 2½-ft. cube |
| Small or Medium | 5 × 5 ft. | 5-ft. cube |
| Large | 10 × 10 ft. | 10-ft. cube |
| Huge | 15 × 15 ft. | 15-ft. cube |
| Gargantuan | 20 × 20 ft. or larger | 20-ft. cube |

For grid calculations, Gargantuan creatures occupy a fixed 4 × 4 × 4 volume.
This is a project interpretation rather than a creature's literal anatomical
height.
